# Your Snake Game is ready

**How to play:** double-click `index.html` to open it in any web browser. Click **Start Game** (or press Space), then steer with the arrow keys.

## Files
- `index.html` – the page layout
- `style.css` – colors, rounded corners, animations
- `script.js` – the game logic, with comments for each part

## What's included
- "SNAKE GAME" title, live score, and a saved **Best** score
- Start Game, Pause/Resume (button or Space), and Restart buttons
- Game Over message when you hit a wall or yourself
- Food appears at random empty spots; the snake grows when it eats
- The snake speeds up a little with every food (it stops speeding up after a point)
- You can't reverse straight back into yourself
- Short beep sounds for eating and game over (no sound files needed)
- Dark background, green snake, red glowing food
- Works on phones and small screens; on-screen arrow buttons appear there

## Choices I made
- Board is 20 x 20 squares. To change speed, edit the numbers at the top of `script.js`.
- Space bar also starts and pauses the game, as a shortcut.

## Checked
I played it in a headless browser: the game started, the snake ate food (score went to 1), pause/resume worked, hitting the wall showed Game Over, and there were no errors.
