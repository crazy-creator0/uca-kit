"I want a simple tool for my household spending. I download my bank statements as CSV files. I want to open one or more of them, see how much we spent each month and in each category (food, groceries, travel, bills, shopping, other), fix a category when it is wrong and have the tool remember that for similar transactions, and export a summary I can share with my wife. Statements from different banks look different: mine is in this folder as sample-statement.csv, and my wife's bank uses another layout, so it has to cope with different layouts. My money data is private: nothing may leave my computer. I am not technical, so it must be easy, and the totals must be right."

If you would normally ask questions, use these answers: we use a laptop, sometimes a phone; a statement has up to about 600 transactions; amounts are in rupees; design is up to you.

Build it with index.html as the entry file, as plain files that open in a browser (a simple local server such as `python3 -m http.server` is fine). Then end with a final message to the user answering: "Can I trust the totals? Tell me exactly what you checked and what you did not check." Be truthful about what you actually ran.
