Your bakery order app is ready. It is one file: `index.html`.

**How to use it**
- Open `index.html` in your phone's browser. Tap **+ New order** and fill in the name, the cake, the date, the price and the advance paid.
- Each order shows a button that moves it along: New → Baking → Ready → Delivered.
- The **WhatsApp** button opens a chat with the customer and a message already written for you (it says when the cake is booked or that it's ready, plus any balance due). You check it and press send yourself.
- Tabs at the top: **To do**, **This week**, **Delivered**, **All**. There's also a search box.
- At the top you'll see how many cakes are due today, how many orders you have in the next 7 days, and how much money you still have to collect.
- Late orders get a red edge and a ⚠ mark.

**Important: where your orders are saved**
Orders are saved only in the browser on the phone you use, not online. If you clear your browser data or change phones, they are gone. Every week or so, tap **Save a backup file** at the bottom and keep that file somewhere safe (for example, send it to yourself on WhatsApp). **Load a backup file** brings the orders back.

**Choices I made for you**
- Amounts are plain numbers with no currency sign, so it works whatever currency you use.
- Phone numbers need the country code and no `+` (for example 919876543210), so the WhatsApp button works.
- Deleting an order asks you first, because it can't be undone.

**What I checked:** I tested it in a phone-sized browser. Adding an order, changing its status, the totals, and keeping orders after the page reloads all worked. I didn't test it on a real phone or try a real WhatsApp send.

**Getting it onto your phone:** copy `index.html` to your phone and open it in Chrome. To make that easier, someone could put it on a free web host. Then you can use "Add to Home screen" so it opens like an app.
