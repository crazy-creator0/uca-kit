# I built your Snake Game, and it works in my tests

**To play:** double-click `index.html` to open it in any web browser. You don't need to install anything or be online.

## What's in the folder
- `index.html`: the page (title, score, board, buttons)
- `style.css`: the look (dark theme, green snake, red food, rounded corners, animations)
- `script.js`: the game logic, split into labelled sections with plain-English comments

## What you get
- **"SNAKE GAME" title**, with your **current score** and **best score** shown. Your best score is saved in the browser.
- **Arrow keys** steer the snake. It moves smoothly between squares instead of jumping.
- **Eating the red food** makes the snake longer, adds 1 point and plays a short "blip". New food appears on a random empty square.
- **Hitting a wall or your own body** ends the game. You hear a falling tone, the board shakes and a **Game Over** message shows your score with a **Play Again** button.
- **Buttons:** Start Game, Pause/Resume and Restart. You can also press **Space or P** to pause.
- **Speed increases** a little with every food you eat, until it hits a top speed.
- **No instant U-turns:** if the snake is going right, pressing left does nothing.
- **Works on any screen size.** Phones and tablets get big on-screen arrow buttons, because they have no keyboard.

## Choices I made for you
- The board is 20 × 20 squares. Each food is worth 1 point.
- Speed: one move every 0.15 s at the start, 0.004 s faster per food, and never faster than one move every 0.06 s. You can change these numbers at the top of `script.js`.
- Extras I added because they're small and help: a **Best** score, and an automatic pause when you switch to another tab or window.
- The sounds are generated by the browser itself, so there are no sound files. Browsers only allow sound after you click something, so sound starts from your first click on Start Game.

## How I checked it
I ran the game in an automated, hidden copy of Chrome and confirmed these all work:
- Starting the game
- Pressing the opposite direction is ignored
- Eating adds a point, makes the snake longer and speeds it up
- Pause really stops the snake, and Resume continues
- Hitting a wall ends the game, and so does hitting the snake's own body
- Restart and the top speed limit
- On a phone-sized screen, the on-screen arrows show and work, and the page doesn't spill off the side
- The page shows no errors

I also looked at screenshots on desktop, phone and a phone held sideways.

Testing caught one real bug, which I fixed. The food's "pop-in" animation could briefly try to draw a circle with a negative size, and that froze the game before the snake ever moved. I also changed the game loop so a drawing glitch like that can't freeze it again.

I could not check whether the sounds are audible, because the test browser has no speakers. Please listen for them when you play.

## What could go wrong
- In some browsers, opening the file directly may not save the **Best** score between visits. The game still works.
- On a very short screen, like a phone held sideways, the board shrinks to fit, so it gets small.
