# Design brief
- Job: see who owes what and mark a month paid.
- Direction: Ledger (paper, ruled lines), changed to a single-column phone list with a monthly stamp.
- Type: Newsreader/Georgia for text, IBM Plex Mono/system mono for money; weights 400, 600; sizes 15, 17, 20, up to 72 for the total.
- Color: paper #F4EFE6, ink #1D1B16, owing red #B3261E, paid green #2F6B3A.
- Space: 8px step, 2px radius, 1px rules, no shadows.
- Layout: pending total leads, month bar next, then the student list.
- Stack: plain HTML, CSS, JavaScript in one file.
- Signature: green PAID stamp tilted on the row.
- Departure: stamp is green for paid, red reserved for money owed.
